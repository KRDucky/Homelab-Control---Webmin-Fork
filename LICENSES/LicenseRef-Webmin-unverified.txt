Licence not stated (Webmin reference tree, third-party material)

Used only for files under compat/webmin/ (unmodified upstream Webmin, not shipped
by Homelab Control) that appear to be third-party material (bundled Perl modules,
icons, small scripts or data sets credited to other authors) but contain no
licence statement, and whose licence could not be established from the tree
itself. Webmin's top-level LICENCE is not assumed to cover them.

Their licence is UNKNOWN to Homelab Control. They must not be copied into, or
distributed with, Homelab Control until their licence has been established from
the original upstream source. Details per file:
docs/compliance/WEBMIN-LICENSE-INVENTORY.md.
